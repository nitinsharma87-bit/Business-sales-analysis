# Business-sales-analysis
Interactive Power BI dashboard for analyzing business sales, profit, products, customer segments, countries, and performance trends.
📊 Business Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard

📌 About the Project

This project transforms raw business sales data into an interactive and easy-to-understand Business Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard using Microsoft Power BI.

The dashboard helps stakeholders understand sales performance, profitability, product performance, customer segments, country-wise sales, and yearly/monthly trends.

🎯 Project Objectives
Analyze overall business sales and profit
Compare sales performance across countries
Identify top-performing products
Analyze profit by customer segment
Understand the impact of discount bands
Compare yearly and monthly performance
Provide interactive filters for business analysis

🔍 Key Insights
The business generated $118.73M in total sales and $16.89M in total profit.
The overall profit margin was 14.23%.
Sales increased significantly from 2013 to 2014.
Paseo recorded the highest product sales.
The United States recorded the highest sales among the countries.
The Government segment generated the highest overall profit.
The Enterprise segment recorded negative overall profit.
Interactive slicers allow users to analyze the dashboard based on different business dimensions.
